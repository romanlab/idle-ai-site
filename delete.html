Idle AI — Delete your data

Delete your Idle AI data

Idle AI is a game by Roman Labunsky. This page explains which data exists, how to delete it yourself, and how to ask us to delete the rest.

There is no Idle AI account. The game never asks you to create an account with us. You have no username or password with us, and the game plays without signing in to anything.

1. Data on your phone

Your save, settings, the game's event log and its diagnostics id live on your phone. To delete them, open Settings → Delete local data and confirm, or uninstall the game. If Android backed up your save to your Google account, you can delete that backup in your phone's backup settings.

2. Analytics and crash data

The game sends gameplay analytics and crash reports to Google's Firebase, linked to a random app-instance id, not to your name. To have that data deleted, write to romalabunsky@gmail.com with the diagnostics id shown under Settings → About (copy it before you delete local data or uninstall). We delete it, or ask Google to delete it, within 30 days of your request.

3. Diagnostics files you sent us

If you sent us a file with Send diagnostics, we delete it within 90 days, or sooner if you ask.

4. Purchases

Google Play processes purchases and keeps its order records under Google's privacy policy. We see each order (order number, product, price and country) in Google Play Console and keep these records only as long as tax law requires. The order numbers stored in your save are deleted with the save.

5. Emails

If you write to us, we keep the conversation until your question is answered and delete it on request.
