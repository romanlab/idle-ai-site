Idle AI — Delete your data

Delete your Idle AI data

Idle AI is a game by Roman Labunsky. This page explains which data exists, how to delete it yourself, and how to ask us to delete the rest.

There is no Idle AI account. The game never asks you to create an account with us. You have no username or password with us, and the game plays without signing in to anything. The only sign-in it can use is your own Google Play Games account, for Cloud save: Play Games may sign you in on its own when the game starts, as it does for other games, and you can sign in under Settings → Cloud save; the game itself never asks you to. We never receive your Play Games name, email address or player id.

1. Your cloud save (Android)

If Cloud save is on, a copy of your save is stored in your own Google Play Games account, so you can continue on another phone. Cloud save appears in Settings only in versions of the game that offer it, and it needs Google Play Games on the phone.

Delete it in the game:

  1. Open Settings → Cloud save.
  2. Tap Delete cloud save, then hold the button to confirm.

This deletes the copy in your Play Games account. The save on your phone stays, and Cloud save stays off on that phone until you turn it on again. If the phone is offline, Cloud save shows Deletion pending, and the deletion runs the next time the phone is online with the game open.

Delete it without the game: cloud saves live in your Google Play Games account, and Google lets you delete the Play Games data of a single game, this game's cloud save included: see Google's help page Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.

2. Data on your phone

Your save, settings, the game's event log and its diagnostics id live on your phone. To delete them, open Settings → Delete local data and confirm, or uninstall the game. If Android backed up your save to your Google account, you can delete that backup in your phone's backup settings.

If Cloud save is on, Delete local data asks you to choose:

  • Delete on this phone only: the cloud save stays in your Play Games account, and Cloud save is turned off on this phone, so the deleted save does not come back on its own.
  • Delete everywhere: the cloud save is deleted first (part 1), then the phone's data.

3. Analytics and crash data

The game sends gameplay analytics and crash reports to Google's Firebase, linked to a random app-instance id, not to your name or your Play Games account. To have that data deleted, write to romalabunsky@gmail.com with the diagnostics id shown under Settings → About (copy it before you delete local data or uninstall). We delete it, or ask Google to delete it, within 30 days of your request.

4. Diagnostics files you sent us

If you sent us a file with Send diagnostics, we delete it within 90 days, or sooner if you ask.

5. Purchases

Google Play processes purchases and keeps its order records under Google's privacy policy. We see each order (order number, product, price and country) in Google Play Console and keep these records only as long as tax law requires. The order numbers stored in your save are deleted with the save, and with the cloud save. Deleting your data does not cancel a purchase: Ad-Free belongs to your Google account and comes back when you reinstall the game, on any phone signed in to that account.

6. Emails

If you write to us, we keep the conversation until your question is answered and delete it on request.
