Idle AI — Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026.

Who we are. Idle AI is made by Roman Labunsky, an individual developer. Contact: romalabunsky@gmail.com.

What this policy covers. The Idle AI app on Google Play and this website.

No account is required. The game never asks you to create an account with us, and it plays fully without signing in to anything. Your progress is stored on your phone, and Android may back it up to your Google account under your phone's backup settings. On Android, versions of the game that offer Cloud save can also keep a copy of your save in your own Google Play Games account (item 9). We never receive your Play Games name, email address or player id.

What we collect and why.

  1. Gameplay analytics (Firebase Analytics, Google). A random app-instance identifier and events about how the game is played: sessions, launches, purchases made, ads watched, screens opened, and performance timings. Purpose: understanding and improving the game; measuring whether the first hour works. Retention: 14 months (Firebase default), then deleted or aggregated.
  2. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Google). Device model, OS version, app version, a crash stack trace and the last few game events before the crash. Purpose: fixing bugs. Retention: 90 days.
  3. Advertising (Google AdMob). When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google's advertising SDK receives your device's advertising identifier and standard device information to serve, measure and (where you have consented) personalise the ad. We show ads only when you tap a button that names the reward; there are no interstitial ads. Where required (EEA, UK, some US states) you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. If you buy Ad-Free, the game does not start the advertising SDK again on that device.
  4. Purchases (Google Play). When you buy something, Google Play processes the payment; we never see your card details. Google Play tells the game which Idle AI products your Google account owns, so the game can give you what you bought. The game keeps the order number of each purchase on your phone, inside your save, so a purchase is never given twice. We see each order in Google Play Console: the order number, the product, the price and your country. The gameplay analytics of item 1 also record each purchase, with its product, price and order number. Your purchases belong to your Google account, so they come back on any phone signed in to it. Retention: Google keeps its order records under its own policy; the order number stays in your save until you delete the save.
  5. Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config, Google). The app fetches balance values; the request carries the app-instance identifier and device locale.
  6. Server time (Google). While the game is open, it sends an HTTPS HEAD request to Google's generate_204 address about every 30 minutes to read the current time, so daily limits are fair. The request carries your IP address and user agent, which Google may log under its own policy. We do not receive these logs.
  7. Website. This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics; GitHub may log visits under its own privacy statement.
  8. Diagnostics you choose to send. The game keeps a log of its recent events (as in item 1) and error notes on your phone; the log is never backed up, and Delete local data removes it. Under Settings → Support → Send diagnostics, the game puts that log in a file together with the diagnostics id, the app version, the device model and the Android version, and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send diagnostics and choose where the file goes. If you send it to us, we use it to fix problems and to see whether the game's first session works, and we delete it within 90 days.
  9. Cloud save (Google Play Games saved games, Android; optional). In versions of the game that offer Cloud save, if you are signed in to Google Play Games and Cloud save is on, the game stores a copy of your save in your own Play Games account, so you can continue on another phone. Play Games may sign you in on its own when the game starts, as it does for other games; the game never asks you to sign in, and you can sign in, turn Cloud save off or delete the cloud copy under Settings → Cloud save. The copy holds your game progress and settings, play times, and the order numbers of purchases already given to you in the game (so a purchase is never given twice). It holds no name, email address, contact details or Play Games id. Google also stores the play time, a short description of your progress and the device name with the copy, and shows them in its saved-games screens. Google holds the copy under its own privacy policy; we do not receive it, and only the game on your devices reads it. So that the game can tell whose save it holds, it keeps on your phone a code made from your Play Games id; the code stays on your phone and in Android's backup of your save, and the game sends it nowhere. The analytics of item 1 record when Cloud save signs in, syncs, settles a conflict between two phones, restores or deletes a save, never with your Play Games id or that code. Purpose: backup and continuing on another device. Retention: until you delete it.

What we do not collect. Name, email (unless you write to us), contacts, location, photos, microphone, or any content you create. We do not sell personal data. We do not use data for advertising profiles outside Google's SDKs described above.

Children. Idle AI is for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the game, write to romalabunsky@gmail.com and we will delete the associated data.

Your choices and rights. You can decline personalised ads in the consent form, or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. You can delete all data on your phone under Settings → Delete local data, or by uninstalling the game. The game has no switch for analytics yet; you can ask us to delete the analytics and crash data linked to your phone. If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California or another place with data-protection rights, you may ask us to access, correct or delete data we hold. Because the game has no account with us, we find your data by the diagnostics id shown under Settings → About, so include it when you write. Where Cloud save is offered, you can turn it off, or delete the cloud copy, under Settings → Cloud save; you can also delete it from Google's Play Games settings. Every way to delete your data, and what we keep, is explained at https://romanlab.github.io/idle-ai-site/delete.html.

Transfers. Google processes data in the United States and other countries under its standard contractual clauses.

Third-party policies. Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Games): policies.google.com/privacy. GitHub (this website): GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes. We will post changes here with a new effective date and, for material changes, a notice in the app.
